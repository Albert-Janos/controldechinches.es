Identification

Could you have bed bugs?

Check the mattress seams and the area around the bed. Bites alone cannot confirm a bed bug infestation.

Itchy bites

Red marks in a line or small groups.

Dark spots

Small marks along mattress seams, the bed frame or near the wall.

Stains on bedding

Small rust-coloured spots on sheets or pillowcases.

Shed skins

Pale, translucent skins near beds, sofas or cracks.

Treatments

Two methods, a tailored plan

We recommend a method after assessing the areas that need treatment.

Combined chemical treatment

Targeted application based on the extent of the infestation, with cold fogging where appropriate. If biocides are used, we explain preparation, ventilation and re-entry.

High-temperature dry steam

Targeted heat for suitable surfaces. We consider dry steam as a complement after assessing the affected areas.
