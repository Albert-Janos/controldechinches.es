Frequently asked questions

Questions for accommodation providers

Preparation, availability and planning the visit.

How soon can you come?

Availability depends on the location, scope and schedule. Tell us the next check-in date and we’ll confirm the first available appointment.

Do I have to close the whole property?

We assess which rooms should be kept vacant based on the signs and the treatment method. Nearby rooms may need inspection. Your technician will explain which areas to restrict.

When can I welcome guests again?

Follow the technician’s instructions on access, ventilation and use of the room. Ask whether a follow-up inspection is advisable before reopening for bookings. Timing depends on the treatment.

How many visits will I need?

This depends on the extent of the problem, the areas affected and what we find after the first visit. Your proposal sets out the planned visits.

How do you price the work?

We assess the affected rooms or units, scope, method, visits and travel. We confirm the price in writing before you book.

Can I request a work report?

Yes, ask when you first get in touch. We’ll confirm what documentation we can supply and what it covers before you book.

Can I get a quote for several apartments?

Yes. Tell us how many units you manage, which show signs and how each can be accessed.

How does follow-up work?

Your proposal states whether follow-up is included. If you spot new signs, tell us where and when so we can assess whether to return.
