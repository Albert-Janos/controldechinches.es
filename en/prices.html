Frequently asked questions

Questions about pricing

How rates work and when we confirm the final price.

How much does bed bug treatment cost?

Chemical treatment starts at €139 for one bedroom, €179 for two, €219 for three and €259 for four; each additional bedroom adds €40. Dry steam as a stand-alone treatment starts at €150 and the final price depends on an on-site assessment.

What is the difference between chemical treatment and dry steam?

Chemical treatment applies products in a targeted way to the affected areas. Dry steam applies high-temperature heat, without insecticides, to compatible surfaces. We assess your case and recommend the right method.

Do I always need two treatments?

One visit may suffice for a localised infestation. If more areas are affected, the technician may recommend a second visit, which is quoted separately.

Is travel included?

Tell us your town so we can confirm coverage and whether the quote includes travel.

How much does steam treatment cost?

Professional dry steam costs an extra €80 per bedroom.

How much does fogging cost?

Cold fogging (ULV) starts at an additional €50 and supplements the main treatment.

Is the calculator price final?

The calculator estimates the price using our published rates. We confirm the final cost, taxes, travel and extras in writing before you book.

How do I check availability?

Availability depends on your town and our schedule. Message us on WhatsApp to ask about a date.
